# Brand clearance — B5

Status: **Preliminary research complete; owner decision and formal legal clearance required.**
Last checked: 2026-10-30

This is a research note, not legal advice. Trademark availability and risk require a
qualified IP attorney and a formal clearance search.

## Candidate name

**Base Case** (working name). It is descriptive of the product's teaching method and
is used as the application name in the repository.

## Preliminary findings

### Trademark / software uses

- **Certara Germany GmbH — BASECASE.** USPTO service mark serial `77955079`, filed
  2010-03-10, covering “teaching and training in business, industry and information
  technology.” Certara markets **BaseCase®** value-communication software at
  `https://www.certara.com/basecase/`; it is listed among Certara's trademarks in its
  10-K filings. This is the most material conflict because it combines a nearly
  identical mark with software and education/training services.
- **BaseCase Inc.** `https://basecase.co` presents “BaseCase — Build Awesome Apps,” a
  software/app-development business.
- **Basecase** at `https://basecase.app` is a task-management software product.
- **Base Case, LLC** at `https://getbasecase.com` operates a commerce site and asserts
  trademark/service-mark rights on its legal page.
- `basecase.com` redirects to Certara's BaseCase product; `basecase.io`,
  `basecase.co`, `basecase.ai`, `basecase.app`, `basecase.net`, `basecase.org`,
  `basecase.xyz`, `basecase.study`, `getbasecase.com`, and `trybasecase.com` are
  registered.

### Domains and handles

- `basecase.dev` had no A record, WHOIS record, or RDAP record when checked on
  2026-10-30. It appears unregistered, but confirm at a registrar; `.dev` WHOIS
  coverage is inconsistent.
- `base-case.dev`, `basecase.tools`, `basecase.practice`, `basecase.run`,
  `basecase.works`, `basecase.software`, and `basecase.page` also showed no DNS/WHOIS
  record; verify each before relying on availability.
- `github.com/basecase` and `github.com/base-case` exist; `github.com/getbasecase` was
  unclaimed. `x.com/basecase` exists; `x.com/basecaseapp` was unclaimed.

## Risk assessment

The name is crowded in software, education/training, and app development. The
Certara registration and the `basecase.co`, `basecase.app`, and `getbasecase.com`
uses make a public launch under “Base Case” risky without written clearance. The
fact that a domain like `basecase.dev` may be available does not resolve the
trademark risk.

Recommended default: **choose a new public brand**, and keep “Base Case” only as an
internal/working name if needed for the private alpha. If the owner wants to keep
the name publicly, obtain a formal clearance opinion and accept the residual risk in
writing before spending on branding, domains, or promotion.

## Immediate owner actions

1. **Decide the brand strategy.**
   - Rename before public branding (recommended), or
   - keep “Base Case” for the private alpha and commission a formal clearance search
     before any public launch.
2. **Commission a trademark clearance search** from an IP attorney for the chosen
   name in the relevant markets (at least US, EU, UK) and classes **9** (software),
   **41** (education/training), and **42** (SaaS/technology services). Ask for:
   - identical and confusingly similar marks;
   - common-law/software-product uses;
   - registrability and likelihood-of-confusion opinion;
   - a written go/no-go recommendation.
3. **Search official databases** for the record: USPTO TESS, EUIPO eSearch, UKIPO,
   and WIPO Global Brand Database. Search both “Base Case” and “BaseCase,” including
   phonetic equivalents.
4. **Choose and reserve the domain** for the selected name. If the name changes,
   reserve the matching `.com`, `.dev`, and `.app` where practical. If the name stays
   for the alpha, confirm and register `basecase.dev` immediately; do not rely on the
   preliminary availability check.
5. **Reserve the matching GitHub organization and social handles.** `github.com/basecase`
   and `x.com/basecase` are already taken.
6. **Record the decision and evidence** in `docs/project/open-blockers.md`:
   chosen name, counsel/search reference, search date, classes/jurisdictions, domain
   registrar and expiry, handles, and any accepted risk.
7. **If renaming:** update `README.md`, app display name, package/bundle identifiers,
   `NOTICE`, `CONTENT_LICENSE`, `pack.json`, attribution strings, screenshots, and
   release docs in one branding change after the name is cleared.

## Exit criteria for B5

- A documented name decision.
- A formal clearance search and written opinion for the chosen public name.
- Primary domain registered and auto-renew enabled.
- Matching GitHub organization and primary social handles reserved.
- Evidence and any accepted exceptions recorded in `docs/project/open-blockers.md`.
